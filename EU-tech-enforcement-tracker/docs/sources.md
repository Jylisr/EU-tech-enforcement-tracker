## Cases identified but excluded from this dataset (out of scope)

The project's scope was expanded (schema v2) from an original five-state pilot to all EU Member States, because GDPRhub does not have enough cases in five states alone to reach a workable sample. One case supplied for consideration remains excluded, on a different and firmer basis than "not yet in scope":

| Reference | What it is | Why excluded |
|---|---|---|
| `EWCA - CA-2025-000651` | *RTM v Bonne Terre (t/a Sky Betting and Gaming)* [2026] EWCA Civ 488 | Decided under **UK GDPR**, not EU GDPR. Post-Brexit, the UK is outside the EU regulatory framework, EU GDPR, and CJEU jurisdiction entirely — this measures a different legal system, not divergence within the one this project studies. See `coding_rules.md` for the full rationale. If UK comparison is wanted later, it belongs in a clearly separated analysis, not the main sample. |
