# EU Tech Enforcement Tracker

A structured dataset and analysis of GDPR enforcement decisions across EU
Member States, examining how Article 82 non-material damages awards and,
separately, how Article 83 administrative fines vary across jurisdictions.

## Purpose

This project tracks enforcement decisions in EU digital regulation
(primarily GDPR) across EU Member States and several digital sectors, to
examine whether enforcement outcomes, particularly damages awards under
Article 82 and administrative fines under Article 83, are consistent across jurisdictions.

## Research Question

To what extent do procedural and institutional differences across Member
States produce divergent enforcement outcomes under EU digital regulation?

## Scope

Covers decisions from EU Member States only. The dataset started as a
five-state pilot (Ireland, Germany, France, Finland, Estonia) and was
expanded (schema v2) to all EU Member States once it became clear
GDPRhub doesn't have enough five-state coverage to reach a workable
sample. **The United Kingdom is deliberately excluded**. Post-Brexit,
the UK applies UK GDPR, a distinct legal regime outside EU law and CJEU
jurisdiction, not simply a state "not yet covered." See
`docs/coding_rules.md` for the full rationale.

States with at least one coded decision so far: Ireland, Germany, France,
Finland, Estonia, Netherlands, Spain, Lithuania, Greece, Italy, Poland,
Hungary.

## Variables

See `docs/coding_rules.md` for full definitions. Summary:

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Unique identifier |
| `member_state` | EU Member State where decision was issued |
| `authority` | Specific DPA or court |
| `ecli` | European Case Law Identifier, where available |
| `year` | Year of decision |
| `sector` | Digital sector (social_media, adtech, ecommerce, etc.) |
| `legal_basis` | GDPR article(s) invoked — GDPR articles only, see coding_rules.md |
| `violation_type` | Category of violation |
| `enforcing_body` | DPA, court_first_instance, court_appeal, or EDPB |
| `damages_awarded` | Art. 82 compensation paid to the data subject (EUR), if any |
| `fine_amount` | Art. 83 administrative fine paid to the state (EUR), if any — kept separate from `damages_awarded`, these are different legal mechanisms |
| `harm_type` | Type of harm (non_material, material, reputational, multiple, none_found, not_yet_decided) |
| `cross_border` | Whether case involved cross-border processing |

## Methodology

Cases are drawn from GDPRhub, national DPA decision registers, and the
EDPB decision register, and coded against explicit rules recorded in
`docs/coding_rules.md`. Every judgment call made during coding is logged in
`docs/coding_log.md` as it happens, which becomes the basis for the paper's
methodology section. Every row's citation and verification status is
tracked in `docs/sources.md`; several rows are flagged there as needing
confirmation against a primary source before being relied on in the paper.

## Dependencies

- R (>= 4.1)
- `tidyverse`
- `ggplot2` (included in tidyverse)
- `scales` (for log-scale fine amount plot)

Install with:

```r
install.packages(c("tidyverse", "scales"))
```

Run the analysis from the repo root:

```r
source("analysis/enforcement_analysis.R")
```

## Status

Active data collection: see `docs/coding_log.md` for progress. Currently
28 decisions across 12 Member States. `analysis/enforcement_analysis.R`
produces the descriptive tables and figures underlying the accompanying
SSRN working paper (draft in `paper/`), but several rows are still marked
unverified in `docs/sources.md` - resolve those before treating any
finding as final.
